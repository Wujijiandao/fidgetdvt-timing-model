# Reproducibility

The public release is intentionally narrower than the private research archive.

Canonical claims reproduced here:
1. Hitos-derived macro sitting-still flow-deficit mapping;
2. held-out hourly-activity prediction;
3. finite-reset robustness outputs;
4. evidence-constrained fast-refill model;
5. manuscript main figures.

Historical exploratory models are excluded from the public repository because
they contain superseded parameterizations and are not required to reproduce the
final claims.
